🛡️ Risk-Based Permission Framework

Not all permissions are equal. Our intelligent risk classification system categorizes every single Odoo permission into four security tiers, ensuring that sensitive system-level access remains protected while empowering safe delegation of routine administrative tasks.
⚠️ Critical

Protected Access

System-level permissions that control the entire Odoo instance. These are automatically hidden from standard role templates and require explicit Super Admin authorization through special workflows.

  • Super Admin access
  • User permission management
  • System configuration
  • Security settings control
🔒 CANNOT be included in role templates or bulk operations
🔴 High Risk

Sensitive Operations

Permissions that can significantly impact business operations or data integrity. Templates containing these require Super Admin approval before activation.

  • Delete critical records
  • Financial transactions
  • Data export capabilities
  • Archive management
✋ Requires Super Admin approval workflow
🟡 Medium Risk

Standard Administrative

Typical business operations that involve editing sensitive data. Permission Managers can assign these, with full audit logging for compliance.

  • Edit patient/customer records
  • View financial reports
  • Assign basic user roles
  • Modify business data
📋 Available in role templates with audit trail
🟢 Low Risk

Routine Access

Everyday operational permissions for reading data and performing basic tasks. Safe for broad assignment and included in all role management features.

  • View dashboards & reports
  • Create appointments
  • Update own profile
  • Read-only data access
✅ Freely assignable through all methods

⚙️ Built-In Safety Controls

🔐

Automatic Protection

Critical permissions are automatically excluded from "Grant All" operations and bulk assignments, preventing accidental privilege escalation.

👥

Safe Delegation

Permission Managers never see Critical-level permissions, allowing secure delegation of administrative duties without risk.

✅

Approval Workflows

Templates containing High-risk permissions automatically trigger Super Admin review before they can be applied to users.

📊

Complete Audit Trail

Every risk-based decision is logged with who, what, when, and why—perfect for ISO 27001, SOC2, and GDPR compliance.

⏱️

Time-Based Expiry

Optional auto-expiration for High-risk permissions ensures temporary access doesn't become permanent security vulnerabilities.

🎯

Separation of Duties

Prevent users from holding conflicting High-risk permissions, maintaining proper checks and balances in your organization.
